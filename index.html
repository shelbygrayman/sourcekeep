Private by design. Clear about limits.

Your documents and searches are processed in this tab and are not uploaded. Closing or refreshing clears the library.

Enabling AI downloads model files from Hugging Face and its delivery hosts, and runtime files from jsDelivr. They receive ordinary connection information, but not document text or search queries. Model files may be cached by your browser. The first download needs an internet connection.

Evidence exports contain your query and selected passages. Review them before sharing. Hashes detect changes; they do not prove truth or authorship.

English text and Markdown are supported. PDFs, OCR, generated answers, and synchronization are outside this first version. Browser extensions and a compromised device remain outside the app’s protection.

Search by meaning, on your device.

Download the quantized Mixedbread embedding model from Hugging Face (roughly 25 MB of weights, plus runtime files). It runs locally using WebGPU or a CPU fallback.

Model loading and indexing can take time. Keyword search remains available if the model cannot run. Meaning-based matches are suggestions, not confidence scores.
